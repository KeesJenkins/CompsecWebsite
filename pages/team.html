Group Guidelines

The following guidelines were agreed upon by all group members at the start of the project.

  1. All members must attend scheduled meetings unless absence is communicated in advance.
  2. Tasks must be completed by the deadlines agreed upon in meetings.
  3. All members should contribute equally to research and writing.
  4. Any major changes to the website must be communicated to the group before being published.
  5. Disagreements should be resolved through group discussion; if unresolved, the Group Leader has final say.
  6. All sources must be properly cited in APA format on the References page.
