Online Censorship

Governments have always tried to control what people can say. What's changed is that the internet made that much harder - and their attempts to reassert control have become one of the defining political conflicts of our time. This site explores how online censorship works, what it costs, and who gets to decide where the line sits.

About This Project

Our research covers the topic from multiple angles - the technical systems governments use to censor the internet, the opportunities open platforms create for ordinary people, the genuine risks that come with both censorship and total openness, and the hard choices that don't have clean answers.

Technology / Topic

An overview of online censorship as a digital system: what it is, how it works, and how social media and digital journalism interact with government control of information.

Opportunities

The positive impacts of open internet access - from strengthening democracy and amplifying citizen movements to enabling a freer press and global communication.

Risks

The dangers of both unchecked internet use and government overreach - including misinformation, hate speech, surveillance, and politically motivated internet shutdowns.

Choices

The ethical, legal, and policy decisions facing governments, technology companies, and individuals in responding to the challenges of online censorship.
